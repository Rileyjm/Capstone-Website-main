Overview

What is DogDecode?

DogDecode is a centralized canine genomics platform built for researchers, veterinarians, students, and dog enthusiasts. It provides a searchable database of SNPs (single nucleotide polymorphisms), genes, dog breeds, and disease associations drawn from published research and curated genomic databases.

The platform lets you explore how specific genetic variants affect coat color, body weight, disease risk, and hundreds of other traits across more than 180 dog breeds.

🔍

🧬

340+ Genes

🐶

Breed-specific variant frequencies, trait markers, and ancestral background data.

❤️‍🩹

Risk factor markers for hereditary conditions with literature citations.

Getting Started

Your First Search

Running a search takes three steps. You can query one data type or combine several in a single search.

  • 1

    Go to the Home page

    The search panel is at the center of the home page under "Explore Genomic Data." You'll see four search types listed: SNP, Gene, Breed, and Disease.

  • 2

    Check one or more search types

    Click the checkbox next to any type you want to search. A text input will appear beneath it. You must fill in every box you check before searching.

  • 3

    Enter your query and hit Search

    Type your search term in the input field and click the blue "Search" button. Results open immediately. Your search is also saved to your profile history automatically.

✅

Combine searches

You can check multiple types at once — for example, search for a Gene and a Breed simultaneously. Results for all checked types appear on the combined results page.

Search Types

What You Can Search

DogDecode supports four distinct data types. Each produces its own detailed results page.

🔍 SNP Search

🔍

Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms

Look up a specific SNP by its reference ID. Results show the chromosomal position, allele frequencies, mutation type, and any phenotype associations recorded in the database.

Example inputs

rs22232384 rs89456123 rs45678234

🧬 Gene Search

🧬

Genes & Genomic Regions

Search by official gene symbol. Results include the gene's full name, chromosomal location, function, associated SNPs, breed-specific variant frequencies, and links to NCBI.

Example inputs

MC1R ASIP FGF5 POMC

🐶 Breed Search

🐶

Dog Breeds & Genetic Markers

Enter a breed name to view its characteristic genetic markers, common trait variants, health predispositions, and how its genome compares to related breeds.

Example inputs

Labrador Retriever Golden Retriever Australian Shepherd

❤️‍🩹 Disease Search

❤️‍🩹

Disease Associations & Risk Factors

Search hereditary conditions to find the genetic markers associated with increased risk, carrier status, or clear status. Includes affected breeds and supporting literature.

Example inputs

Hip Dysplasia Progressive Retinal Atrophy Von Willebrand Disease
ℹ️

Case sensitivity

Searches are not case-sensitive. "mc1r", "MC1R", and "Mc1r" all return the same results. Gene symbols are conventionally written in uppercase (e.g., MC1R) and breed names in title case.

Results Pages

Reading Your Results

Each search type opens a dedicated results page with several information cards. Here is a breakdown of what each section contains.

SNP Results

The SNP page shows Variant Information (ID, position, mutation type, alleles), Allele Frequencies across breeds, Phenotype Associations (traits linked to this variant), and Related Genes that contain or are near the SNP.

Gene Results

The Gene page shows Gene Information (symbol, ID, chromosome, protein length), Associated SNPs within the gene with their mutation types and MAF values, Breed-Specific Variants with frequency data, Gene Statistics (known SNPs, publications), and Related Genes in the same pathway.

Breed Results

The Breed page shows Breed Overview (origin, size, group), Top Genetic Markers characteristic to the breed, Health Predispositions based on marker frequencies, and Ancestral Composition showing genetic similarity to related breeds.

Disease Results

The Disease page shows the Causative or Associated Genes, Diagnostic Markers used to determine risk, carrier, or clear status, Most Affected Breeds with prevalence rates, and Literature References linking to published research.

⚠️

For research and education only

DogDecode is an informational tool. Results should not replace veterinary genetic testing or professional diagnosis. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for health-related decisions.

Profile

Your Profile Page

The Profile page provides a sample view of what an individual dog's genomic report looks like. Navigate to it via the "Profile" link in the top navbar.

Overview Tab

The Overview tab shows:

  • 🧬

    Genetic Markers

    Key gene variants identified in the sample, including the observed genotype (e.g., A/A, G/T) and the associated trait.

  • ❤️

    Health Associations

    Disease risk assessments derived from known markers. Status badges indicate Low Risk, Carrier, or Clear for each condition.

  • 🐶

    Breed Composition

    A percentage breakdown of the dog's genetic ancestry across identified breeds, shown as proportional bars.

Search History

Viewing Past Searches

Every time you run a search from the home page, it is automatically saved to your browser's local storage — no account required.

  • 1

    Open the Profile page

    Click "Profile" in the navbar or navigate to Profile.html.

  • 2

    Click the "Search History" tab

    The tab bar sits just below the profile hero. Click "Search History" to switch from the Overview tab.

  • 3

    Browse or re-open past searches

    Each history entry shows the search type, query term, and how long ago it was run. Clicking an entry reopens that result page directly.

✅

Clearing history

Use the "Clear All" button in the Search History tab to remove all saved searches. History is stored in your browser only — clearing browser data will also remove it.

Tips & Tricks

Getting the Most Out of DogDecode

  • ✔ Use official gene symbols — Enter the standard symbol like MC1R rather than the full name "Melanocortin 1 Receptor" for the most accurate gene results.
  • ✔ Search multiple types together — Checking both Gene and Breed at once lets you see how a specific gene behaves across a particular breed in one view.
  • ✔ Follow "Related Genes" links — Gene result pages list related genes with pathway similarity scores. These are great starting points for deeper research.
  • ✔ Click "NCBI Gene" on result pages — Each gene page has an external link to the NCBI entry for the full reference sequence, expression data, and primary literature.
  • ✔ Check your search history — The Profile → Search History tab keeps your last 100 searches as clickable links, so you never have to retype a query.
  • ✔ Use the back link — Every result page has a "← Back to Search" link in the top-left that returns you to the home search panel.
